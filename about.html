About Us

Q-Lab is the quantum-computing-focused student club of Government College of Engineering, Erode. The club brings together students and faculty interested in quantum computing and emerging quantum technologies through learning, projects, and collaboration.

Mission

To foster quantum literacy, hands-on learning, interdisciplinary collaboration, and innovation by engaging students in quantum computing and emerging quantum technologies; to develop practical solutions to real-world engineering and industrial challenges through projects, research, mentorship, and industry collaboration.

Quantum Club, Government College of Engineering, Erode

Vision

To empower students to explore quantum solutions for emerging challenges in engineering, computing, and advanced technology.
